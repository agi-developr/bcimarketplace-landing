# BCIMarketplace — Hackathon Submission

## One-liner

BCIMarketplace is an AI-assisted procurement layer for neurotech: it turns fragmented EEG and fNIRS specs into buyer-readable comparisons, collects technical requirements, and routes high-intent demand to the right vendors.

## Problem

Buying neurotech is painful. Labs, XR teams, and startups compare scattered vendor docs, unclear SDK support, gated pricing, and mismatched device categories. Procurement is slow and bad-fit purchases are common.

## Solution

We built a vendor-neutral front end for discovery plus a concierge layer for routing:

- Structured vendor intelligence for EEG and fNIRS devices
- Live comparison pages with practical decision criteria
- RFQ intake that captures technical constraints once
- Vendor onboarding for warm lead routing

## Demo flow

1. Open `/demo.html`
2. Open `/compare/openbci-vs-emotiv.html`
3. Show the RFQ flow at `/rfq.html`
4. Show vendor onboarding at `/vendor-onboard.html`

## Why this is interesting

This is not a generic content site and not a traditional marketplace. It is a procurement intelligence layer: useful before a transaction, useful without holding inventory, and compounding as vendor intelligence grows.
